# Past Exam V3 GOLD — 31-package pre-run inventory and five-sample selection

## Scope and reading rule

This appendix records the 31 existing FAIL/HOLD or external-review packages that were inventoried before the five fresh GOLD attempts. Every row is a high-school grade 1, second-semester mathematics exam. `Included/total` is the old package's included question count over its source denominator; it is not a fresh GOLD result. The old package state is recorded as `NOT_SEALED` because the prior material was comparison-only and did not constitute a current V3 promotion decision.

The five selected runs were rebuilt from the original PDF/HWP source after this inventory. Prior JS, answer, solution, asset, reviewer, and provider evidence was not used as fresh input. The old material was inspected only after the corresponding `GOLD_RESULT_FROZEN` file had been written.

## Inventory interpretation

- The old inventory surfaced all requested dimensions: identity (school/year/grade/term/subject), question denominator, old included/excluded state, source-fidelity risk, solution-quality sealing, choices/image-only structure, visual/SVG demand, subjective workload, and metadata/taxonomy sealing.
- The 31 rows are not evidence that any old package is suitable for promotion. They are the failure-surface sampling frame for the five fresh GOLD attempts.
- The fresh GOLD denominator is determined by each original PDF/HWP and its own frozen source inventory. It is not inherited from the old `Included` column.
